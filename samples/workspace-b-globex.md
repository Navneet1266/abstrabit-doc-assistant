# Globex Inc — Onboarding Handbook

## Company overview

Globex Inc is a logistics analytics company founded in 2014. Its flagship
product, **RouteSense**, helps freight companies predict delivery delays.
The current VP of Engineering is Daniel Okafor.

## Office access

The main office in Austin uses a keycard system. Visitors must be signed in
at the front desk and are issued a temporary badge that expires after 8
hours. The Wi-Fi guest network password is **globex-guest-2024**.

## Vacation policy

Full-time employees accrue 1.5 days of paid time off per month, capped at
18 days per year, and unused days roll over up to a maximum of 10 days into
the following year.
